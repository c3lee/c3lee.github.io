About

Researching the systems behind the future.

I’m Claire Lee — a writer, researcher, and public-interest thinker focused on how government, defense, education, and technology shape one another. My work sits at the intersection of policy analysis, public communication, and digital strategy.

I hold a BA in Political Science and Economics, and an MA in Middle East Policy. I’m especially interested in translating complex systems into clear, relevant narratives for both audiences and institutions.

My work explores how technology can strengthen public institutions — while also revealing the risks, tradeoffs, and opportunities embedded in that change.
